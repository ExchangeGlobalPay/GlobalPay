Withdrawal Guide

1

Open the Wallet Section

Log in to the app and tap on the Wallet section from the bottom menu.

2

Tap on Withdrawal

In the Wallet section, find the Withdrawal option and tap on it to open the withdrawal page.

3

Check Withdrawal Limits

On the Withdrawal page, you can see the available balance. The minimum withdrawal is ₹500 and the maximum withdrawal is ₹50,000.

4

Select Bank or UPI

Choose how you want to receive your money. Select either Bank or UPI and continue.

5

Fill in Your Details

Enter the required details carefully. For Bank, enter your bank details. For UPI, enter your valid UPI ID. Then enter the withdrawal amount and tap Continue.

6

Withdrawal Request Successful

After submitting the request, your Withdrawal Request Successful message will appear. Your request will remain Pending until it is processed.

7

Receive Your Money

After the withdrawal is approved and processed, the money will be sent to your selected Bank Account or UPI. Processing may take a few hours.

8

Check Withdrawal History

You can check your Withdrawal History to see the current status. It will show Pending while processing and Success after completion.
