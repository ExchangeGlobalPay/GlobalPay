Buy & Claim Token Guide

1

Open Buy Token

Log in to GlobalPay and open the Buy Token section. Here you can view the available plans and token options.

2

Choose Your Plan

Select the plan that suits you. Check the price, validity, daily token and total token before continuing.

3

Check Plan Details

Review the selected plan carefully. Make sure the displayed plan details are correct before confirming the purchase.

4

Confirm Your Purchase

After checking the details, tap Continue Buy to complete the purchase. Follow the instructions shown on the screen.

5

Check My Plans

After a successful purchase, open My Plans. Your active plan and its available daily token information will be displayed there.

6

Claim Your Token

When your daily claim becomes available, open My Plans and tap Claim Token. The available token reward will then be added to your wallet according to the plan.

7

Check Your Wallet

After claiming, open your Wallet and check the updated token balance and transaction history.
