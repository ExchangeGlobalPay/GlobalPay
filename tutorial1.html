Add Token Guide

1

Open the Wallet Section

Log in to the app and tap on the Wallet section from the bottom menu.

2

Tap on Add Token

In the Wallet section, you will see the Add Token option. Tap on it.

3

Select the Amount

On the Add Token page, select your desired amount, such as ₹300, ₹500, ₹1,000, etc.

4

QR Code Will Appear

After selecting the amount, the QR Code will appear below. Scan the QR code to make the payment.

5

Make the UPI Payment

Use Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe, Navi, or any supported UPI app to complete the payment.

6

Payment Completed

After the payment is successful, a Payment Successful message will appear on the screen.
